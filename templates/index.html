    How the score works

    Each indicator is scored from −2 to +2 using a contrarian framework: fear / stress favors entry; greed / complacency does not.

    • VIX — VIX > 30 is an entry point (+2). Low VIX (complacency) scores negative.
    • RSP RSI(14) — equal-weight S&P: ≤30 oversold +2 · ≤40 +1 · 40–60 0 · 60–70 −1 · ≥70 overbought −2.
    • Fear & Greed — lower readings favor entry; extreme greed scores negative.
    • AAII — high bearish % (or deeply negative bull−bear spread) is a buy signal.
    • NAAIM — 40–64% = +1 (add risk) · 64–<98% = 0 Strong hold mode · 98–100% = −1 room to take profit · <40% = +2 · >100% = −2.

    Educational / research tool only — not investment advice. Sentiment indicators are noisy and work best as context, not a standalone timing system.
